1. Wells JN & Feschotte C (2020). "A Field Guide to Eukaryotic Transposable Elements". <i>Annual Review of Genetics</i>, 54:539-561. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-genet-040620-022145

    https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-genet-040620-022145

    <b>A comprehensive review of TEs where the authors provide a breakdown of their classification, evolution, and distribution.</b>

2. Storer JM, Hubley R, Rosen J, Smit AFA (2021) "Curation guidelines for de novo generated transposable element families" <i>Current Protocols</i>. 2021. https://doi.org/10.1002/cpz1.154

    https://dfam.org/assets/publications/2021-Current_Protocols-Curation_guidelines.pdf

    <b>A protocol and suggested guidelines for curating models produced by a <i>de novo</i> program.</b>

3. Goubert C, Craig R.J., Bilat A.F., Peona V, Vogan A.A., Protasio A.V. (2022). "A beginner’s guide to manual curation of transposable elements". <i>Mobile DNA</i> 13, 7. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13100-021-00259-7

    https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13100-021-00259-7#citeas

    <b>An excellent guide for beginners on curating TEs. Supplemental information includes command line help and videos for ease of use.</b>

4. Storer JM, Hubley R, Rosen J, & Smit AFA (2022). "Methodologies for the De novo Discovery of Transposable Element Families". <i>Genes</i>, 13(4), 709. https://doi.org/10.3390/genes13040709

    https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4425/13/4/709

     <b>A review of the current TE automatic annotation pipelines currently available (in 2022), as well as a look into how each program contributes to the overall pipeline.</b>

6. Flynn JM, Hubley R, Goubert C, Rosen J, Clark AG, Feschotte C, & Smit AF (2020). RepeatModeler2 for automated genomic discovery of transposable element families. <i>Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America</i>, 117(17), 9451–9457. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1921046117

    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7196820/pdf/pnas.201921046.pdf

     <b>How the RepeatModeler2 program works and a benchmark on multiple species.</b>

6. A.F.A. Smit, R. Hubley & P. Green RepeatMasker at http://repeatmasker.org. RepeatMasker documentation.

    https://www.repeatmasker.org/webrepeatmaskerhelp.html

     <b>A manual for the use of RepeatMasker</b>
